case-study/alerting-as-code
03 · Kubenine (client) · 2026

610 alarms vs 81: rebuilding production alerting as code, then removing the noise

problem:The new production account was almost blind, and the old alerting it was meant to copy was so noisy that engineers had learned to ignore it.

A newly built production account went live with 81 alarms where the old one had 610. I rebuilt coverage as code across CloudWatch, EventBridge, GuardDuty and Prometheus, added a Terraform guard that fails the plan when something is unmonitored, and then tuned the noise by replaying real alert history instead of guessing thresholds.

04 · Kubenine · 2026

One hot pod, four idle ones: fixing Kubernetes traffic skew on EKS

problem:HPA tripled the deployment and added nodes, yet a single pod still did all the work and throttled p99.

One replica pinned at 90%+ CPU while identical pods idled, and HPA scaling out made the bill worse without fixing it. Traced it to kube-proxy's Layer 4 routing combined with keep-alive connection pools, then fixed it at three levels.
